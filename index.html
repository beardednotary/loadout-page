Questions

Before you ask

How is this different from a tab manager?

Tab managers save whatever you happened to have open. LoadOut starts from the project: the links you always need, a saved set of tabs you open on purpose, and the one next thing to do. Saved tabs are one part of it, not the whole product.

Does it replace my new tab page?

Yes, that's where it lives. Chrome asks you to confirm the change the first time you open a new tab. You can go back any time by turning the extension off.

Where does my data live?

In Chrome's storage on your computer, and nowhere else. There's no account and no sync. Export a backup file from Settings, and import it to move to another computer.

Which browsers does it work in?

LoadOut is built and tested for Chrome on desktop. Other Chromium browsers, such as Edge and Brave, should work too.

Is it free?

Yes. Everything in LoadOut is free today. A paid Pro plan will come later, alongside the free plan, and everyone who joins early access gets Pro free, for life, as a thank-you for helping shape it.

How do I get it?

Install it free from the Chrome Web Store. To lock in founding-member Pro, also join early access with your email below.
